# Youth & Work Pakistan: LFS 2024-25 province dashboard

Interactive dashboard of youth schooling, work and labour-force indicators for Pakistan's provinces, comparing the PBS Labour Force Survey 2024-25 with 2020-21.

**Live:** https://danyalkhattak-git.github.io/youth-work-pakistan/

Indicators: not in work or education (15-24), youth unemployment, women in the labour force, out of school and working teens (15-17), unpaid housework and care, and the girls-boys NEET gap. All estimates are survey-weighted from PBS microdata; `prep/lfs_province_prep.py` builds them (raw data not included).
