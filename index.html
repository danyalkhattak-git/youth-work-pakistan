Source and method

Source: Afghanistan Health Cluster 4W partner reports, Jan 2022 – Jul 2023 (72,820 rows, 50 partners, 2,066 facilities). Cleaning: exact duplicates and rows without a total removed; indicator names reported two ways merged; "Sar-e-Pul" spellings merged; indicators reported only as zeros and minor indicators (<1% of rows) left out. A value is flagged when it is at least 500 and more than 10 times the same facility's median for that indicator (facilities with 3+ reports). Timeliness uses the upload date against the last day of the reporting month; the 15-day window is a working assumption. Province boundaries: geoBoundaries (CC BY 4.0). Built by Danyal Khattak.
