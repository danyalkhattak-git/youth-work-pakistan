Source: Pakistan Bureau of Statistics, Labour Force Survey 2024-25 and 2020-21 microdata; weighted estimates by Danyal Khattak.
Method and caveats Estimates use LFS expansion weights; 95% confidence intervals are design-based (enumeration blocks as clusters; province × rural/urban strata in 2024-25, districts in 2020-21). The 2024-25 public file covers ages 15-64, so teen indicators use ages 15-17. Employed = worked for pay, ran a business, helped in a family business, or had a job but was temporarily absent. "Not in work or education" does not count training. Youth unemployment follows the ILO definition (no work, available, and seeking work or due to start one); 2020-21 availability codes differ slightly. LFS 2020-21 was collected during COVID-19 school disruption, so part of the change since 2020-21 may reflect that. People affected are weighted population estimates.
